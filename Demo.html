A Online Base Learning Platform

An online learning platform is a digital environment that offers courses
resources, and tools to facilitate education and skill development
through the internet These platforms provide flexible learning opportunities, enabling students
to access materials such as videos, quizzes, assignments, and interactive content.
Online learning platforms cater to a wide range of subjects, from academic courses to professional
skills, and often include features like forums, live sessions, and certification upon completion.
Popular examples include Coursera, edx, and udemy.
